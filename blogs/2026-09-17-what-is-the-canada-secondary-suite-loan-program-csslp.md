The Canada Secondary Suite Loan Program (CSSLP) no longer exists. Ottawa created it in Budget 2024, doubled the loan to $80,000 in the 2024 Fall Economic Statement, promised a launch in early 2025, then confirmed in Budget 2025 that the program was never made operational and would not be implemented. No application ever opened.
